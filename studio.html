MORE THAN A STUDIO

We're building a home for faith-driven creatives.

202One isn't simply a place to hire equipment. It's a place where musicians, producers, artists, storytellers, engineers and builders meet, create, learn and collaborate. Come in for a two-hour rehearsal, leave having met a producer. A worship team can record a live session. A young artist can work alongside someone more experienced. A filmmaker can find a musician here.

That's the community.
